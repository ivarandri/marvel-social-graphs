The dataset

The course hands every group an identical frozen snapshot (built 26 Aug 2026 from the live English Wikipedia): a node roster of all 303 characters and a directed edge list — an edge A → B means the running text of A's article links to B's. Redirects are resolved on both ends; navbox links are excluded. The files live in data/; the analysis that builds each post lives in analysis/.
