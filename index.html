The dataset

Every group gets an identical frozen snapshot, built 26 Aug 2026 from the live English Wikipedia: a roster of all 303 characters and a directed edge list, where an edge A → B means the running text of A's article links to B's. Redirects are resolved on both ends; navigation-box links are excluded on purpose.
